# Phase 7.14 — Pilot Daily Log Template

One entry per day for 14 days. Keep in your pilot records folder, suggested
`runs/T2/phase7/7.14/pilot/`. Do not commit completed logs.

Times are in **seconds** unless stated. Estimate honestly; a rough number recorded every day is worth
far more than an exact number recorded twice.

**These metrics describe how the toolkit felt to use. They never describe business results, and
nothing here establishes business causation.**
